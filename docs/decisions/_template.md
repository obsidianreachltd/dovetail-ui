# D0000 — Short title of the decision

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | D0000 |
| Status | Proposed |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Context

What problem or question led to this decision. Keep it short.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, then any detail.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to do.

## Alternatives considered

- Each other option, and why it was not chosen.
