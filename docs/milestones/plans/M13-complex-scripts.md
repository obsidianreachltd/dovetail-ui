## Goal

Shape and lay out text in scripts whose letters change form or whose direction runs right to left, starting with Arabic.

## Starting point

- **`dovetail-text`** (M06):
  - reads TrueType and CFF fonts, with fallback between fonts;
  - shapes one line by mapping each character to a glyph, with kerning from `GPOS` (type 2) or `kern`;
  - `ShapedLine` holds glyphs in text order, with each glyph's pen position.
- **Carets and selection** come from `caret_x` and `byte_at`, which assume text runs left to right. They are used by:
  - layout's `Measure` trait, for the caret and selection rectangles and for clicks;
  - `TextField`, which moves the caret in text order;
  - the AccessKit text ranges.
- **Unicode tables** are generated by `tools/ucd-gen` from the UCD (D0014). The bidirectional and joining data is not in them yet.
- **Fonts with Arabic:**
  - Segoe UI and Arial on Windows, already in the system font list;
  - Geeza Pro on macOS, which must be added to the list;
  - Noto Sans Arabic or DejaVu Sans on Linux.
- **Decided on 2026-11-23** ([D0018](../../decisions/D0018-complex-script-foundations.md)):
  - the whole bidirectional algorithm;
  - paragraph direction from the first strong letter, with right-to-left paragraphs lined up on the right;
  - one caret, moved in visual order.
- **UCD files downloaded on 2026-11-23**, as D0014 allows, into `tools/ucd/data/17.0.0/`:
  - `DerivedBidiClass.txt`, `BidiBrackets.txt` and `BidiMirroring.txt`;
  - `ArabicShaping.txt` and `DerivedJoiningType.txt`;
  - `BidiTest.txt` and `BidiCharacterTest.txt`.

## Prototypes and open decisions

- **Bidirectional scope:** decided, the whole algorithm (D0018).
- **Paragraph direction:** decided, from the first strong letter (D0018). A setting to force a direction is a follow-up.
- **Caret:** decided, one caret, visual moves (D0018).
- **Line breaks** in multi-line text start new paragraphs, as UAX #9 says for paragraph separators. Lines are not wrapped, so L1 only has to reset trailing white space.
- **Segoe UI's lam-alef** is two joined glyphs made by chained-context rules, not the single glyph of its presentation forms. Windows' own shaper gives the same two glyphs, so the tests check against it.
- **Follow-ups, after M13:**
  - a setting to force a paragraph's direction (D0018);
  - `GPOS` cursive attachment (`curs`), which Nastaliq fonts need;
  - moving by word in visual order: Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right still move in text order;
  - a right-to-left text box in a `Row` or `Column` that places its children at the start still goes at the start;
  - screen reader checks of right-to-left text runs, with M07's manual checks.

## Risks

- **Fonts differ:** glyph numbers in tests depend on the font. Tests that check glyph sequences look the glyphs up by name or by the font's own forms (`isol`, `init` and so on) for Segoe UI, and are skipped where it is missing.
- **The bidirectional tests are large:** about 15 MB. Only a sample is committed; the whole files are run where they have been downloaded.
- **Shaping cost:** shaping runs again for every measure. The fonts cache the work they can, and the M06 caret and click code is changed to shape each line once.

## Testing

- **Unit tests** for `GSUB`, `GPOS` marks, joining and the bidirectional rules.
- **Conformance:** `BidiTest.txt` and `BidiCharacterTest.txt`, every case, where downloaded; a sample everywhere.
- **Glyph sequences** for Arabic words in Segoe UI on Windows.
- **Carets and selection** in `TextField`, without a window and in its self-test.
- **Sharpness** at three display scales, like M06.
- **By hand:** reading Arabic in the text-field example, on each system. Not done yet.
- **Results on 2026-11-23**, Windows 11:
  - 242 workspace tests pass, every GPU test passes, and every example's self-test passes;
  - a picture from `dovetail-render`'s snapshot example at 200% shows Arabic with vowel marks joined and in order, the marks on their letters, lam-alef joined, and Arabic in an English line in the right order, brackets included.

## Done when

Copied from the overview.

1. Arabic words join correctly (isolated, initial, medial and final forms, and the lam-alef ligature), checked against known glyph sequences for a system font.
2. A line that mixes Arabic and English shows each run in the right order, checked against the bidirectional algorithm's test data for the cases supported.
3. The caret moves and selection works in mixed-direction text, in visual order.
4. Arabic text is sharp at 100%, 150% and 200% display scale.
